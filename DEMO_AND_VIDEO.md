# Turnout: Demo Data, Judge Walkthrough, and Video Script

---

## 1. Synthetic dataset

Generated by `data/generate.py`, seeded, committed as JSON so judges see the same scenario we recorded.

### 1.1 Departments

| Department | Members | Districts | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Millbrook Volunteer Fire Company | 14 | north, south | The department the story follows. Chief Dana Ortiz. Thin daytime coverage. |
| Riverton Fire and Rescue | 22 | central, east | Combination department with two daytime duty crew members. Chief has auto-approval for delays under 10 minutes and ledger within 6 hours. |
| Cedar Hollow Volunteer Fire Department | 11 | west | Weakest department. At high risk in the same window as Millbrook on demo day. |

### 1.2 Members

Each member has a name, phone (sandbox numbers), roles drawn from {firefighter, driver_operator, emt, officer}, certification records with expiry dates (two Millbrook EMT cards expire within 60 days), quiet hours (most 22:00 to 06:00; three members have day-shift quiet hours), and a response history that produces realistic per-window probabilities: retirees high on weekdays, commuters low on weekdays and high on evenings and weekends.

### 1.3 Call history

Twelve months per department, sampled from an hour-of-day and day-of-week profile shaped like the NFPA national pattern (peaks at 10:00 to 12:00 and 17:00 to 19:00, weekday daytime medical-heavy), with monthly seasonality and a small number of time-critical types. Each call records which members responded, consistent with their availability patterns, so response probabilities learned from history are coherent with availability.

### 1.4 The demo week

The scenario file `scenarios/demo_week.json` sets the clock to Wednesday, September 10, 2026, 06:30, and includes a National Weather Service ice storm warning for Thursday from 06:00 to 14:00 (injected through the weather tool's fixture mode; the live NWS tool is used when no fixture is present).

Planted gaps:

| Window | District | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Thu 10:00 to 14:00 | Millbrook north | Critical. Closer fails. Riverton offers. Cedar Hollow declines. Chief approves. The headline gap. |
| Thu 14:00 to 17:00 | Millbrook south | High. Closer succeeds with one targeted ask (a retiree). No chief involvement. |
| Fri 09:00 to 12:00 | Millbrook north | Elevated. Swap proposed between two members and accepted. No chief involvement. |
| Sat 02:00 to 05:00 | Millbrook south | Low. Logged only. |
| Thu 10:00 to 14:00 | Cedar Hollow west | High. Cedar Hollow's own agent handles it; this is why it declines Millbrook. |

Planted incident: Thursday 11:52, motor vehicle collision, Millbrook north, Riverton responds under the confirmed coverage. A synthetic voice note (recorded by us) is included for Scribe.

Planted cert issue: one Millbrook EMT card expires October 30; Cert Clock proposes a refresher on October 4.

## 2. Judge walkthrough (Judge mode, no login)

Designed to take four minutes and to touch every criterion.

1. Open the live URL. Read the hero. Press "Try the live demo."
2. Judge mode loads Millbrook at Wednesday 06:30. Press "Play the week." The clock advances in accelerated time with a visible speed control.
3. Station Board: Thursday 10 to 2 north turns red. Hover the badge to see the risk formula and numbers.
4. Phones panel: watch Roll Call polls go out and replies come back. Watch Closer's two targeted asks and the declines.
5. Network View: watch the A2A request travel to Riverton and Cedar Hollow, Riverton's offer come back, Cedar Hollow's decline with its reason.
6. Chief's phone: one message arrives. Press 1.
7. Station Board: the window turns green with "Covered by Riverton, 9 min." Ledger updates on both sides.
8. Advance to Thursday 11:52. The incident fires. Press "Play debrief." Scribe's NERIS draft appears with two fields marked uncertain. Press "Review."
9. Trace Viewer: open the run. Expand the risk engine call to see inputs and outputs. Expand the A2A exchange.
10. Press "Reset scenario." Optionally, open Developers, copy the sandbox key, and run the curl example for coverage.

Everything above is real system behavior on the deployed stack. The only fixture is the weather alert, and the page says so.

## 4. Recording checklist

- Record from the deployed URL in a clean browser profile, 1920 by 1080, 125 percent zoom for legibility.
- Light theme for the main run; a 5-second dark theme and mobile montage near the end.
- Reset the scenario before recording. Record the whole "Play the week" in one take; cut later.
- Captions from the script, checked against audio.
- Export at 4:15 to 4:30. Upload to YouTube as public. Verify playback while logged out.
